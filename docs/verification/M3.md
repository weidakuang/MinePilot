# M3 Companion Verification

## Exit criteria

The companion must survive a 100-hour world and pass the scenario matrix for
chat, follow, waypoint travel, inventory ownership, construction, farms,
transport, PVE/PVP, recovery, and supported workstation/mod adapters.

The gate also requires at least 50 companion cases, 30 building cases, three
unseen variants for every required farm or machine capability, restart and
chunk-unload checks, 10,000 waypoints, 100,000 assets, and the documented query
p95 budgets.

## Current result

`NOT_RUN`. The SPI, UI, MCP, memory, and many atomic skills exist, and isolated
no-model physical checks are retained as lower-bound evidence. No complete
natural-world companion archive or 100-hour soak has been accepted.

The fail-closed protocol in [`e2e/m3_protocol.py`](../../e2e/m3_protocol.py)
binds each shard to one exact product jar, source commit, real dedicated server,
normal client, configured model, and independent read-only Observer result. A
missing resource, mismatched artifact, incomplete case, or non-terminal shard
returns `NOT_RUN` rather than PASS.

Complex Create, MTR, Farmer's Delight, and aircraft behavior remains behind the
versioned adapter SPI. It is not part of the first M3 claim until a precise
Forge 65.x build and contract test are available.

## Professional-companion increment

The current development line adds two bounded interaction guarantees:

- Skill lifecycle messages are derived from accepted `SkillSupervisor`
  transitions, not from model speech. Duplicate audit notices are suppressed,
  while distinct skills in one goal remain visible.
- Explicit `stop`, `hold`, and `pause` requests use the local
  goal cancellation path. They do not wait for a model round trip, bypass the
  evaluation lock, or directly teleport/remove the body; the active skill must
  reach its ordinary safe checkpoint.

Focused JVM/source-contract tests cover the parser, status wording, duplicate
boundary, and permission-safe conversation path. This is an implementation
increment only: it does not promote the M3 result above `NOT_RUN` until the
real dedicated-server/client/model scenario archive and long soak are present.
